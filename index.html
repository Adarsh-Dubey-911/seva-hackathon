Key Platform Features

🗺 Collision Alert: GIS Map & 500 m / 180-day Clash DetectionVisualizes scheduled excavations across 16 Navi Mumbai, Panvel, and BMC road corridors on real OpenStreetMap street geometry, automatically flagging utility work zones within a 500 m radius and a rolling 180-day planning window.
⚡ Dig Once Trench Merger & Engineering RecommendationsEvaluates PNGRB Gas-to-Power 1.5m safety clearances, new-road protection, and utility depths to suggest optimal fixes—shared trenching, opposite-shoulder co-scheduling, or back-to-back sequencing.
🛣 New-Road Protection & Clearance IDA newly laid road is closed to planned digs for its first year (BMC trench-policy rule). A dig inside that window is flagged: dig before resurfacing, go trenchless (HDD), or pay the early-dig multiplier. Every approved dig gets a NagarSync clearance ID (NS-CLR-…).
₹ SSR-Based Cost & Savings Engine (indicative Maharashtra PWD / NMMC rates)Calculates itemized standalone and avoided excavation, bedding, Asphalt/CC M40 restoration, and traffic diversion costs for every permit application and resolved clash.
📝 RoW Permit Gate, Audit Trail & Public QR BarricadesProvides a pre-application clash check for utility officers, one-click WhatsApp/Email agency alerts, a downloadable Excel audit workbook and CSV audit trail, live Google Sheet/GeoJSON sync, and a read-only Citizen view where every work site has a QR barricade page with a WhatsApp report link.
